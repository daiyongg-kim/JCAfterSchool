A Treasure to Share — Yooha's Bilingual Book of Ten Stories 📖

JC After School · June 4, 2026

Sending blessings to all our students and their families for this final month of June, 2026. Wishing you all well in your exams. 🙏

And today, we have a treasure to share with everyone. It is a book written by our very own student at JC Afterschool this year — a bilingual book that she creatively wrote and designed in the after school academy. It is a collection of ten short stories, written in both English and Korean, and it turned out beautifully!

Well done, Yooha 🌹

Funny Animal Stories for Kids — by Yooha

From a fox cubs' Christmas gone wrong to a K-Pop hamster and a very dramatic chicken race, every story was written, illustrated, and laid out by Yooha herself — each one in English and Korean. Here's what's inside:

  7. Dog's Dangerous Soccer Lesson
  8. The Cat's Worst Day
  9. Thanksgiving Turkey's Escape
  10. The Cat and the Chicken's Race

This is what we love most about our creative writing program — when grammar lessons, idioms, and storytelling come together into something a child can hold in her hands and be proud of for years. Congratulations, Yooha. We can't wait to see what you write next. ❤️
