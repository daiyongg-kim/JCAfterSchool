🍪 About Snacks

We provide a snack every day. If your child is a particularly picky eater, an extra snack from home is always welcome. For everyone's safety, we never prepare peanuts or tree nuts of any kind.

⚠️

If your child has an allergy, please contact us directly. Let us know in advance and we will handle meals and snacks with care. (Call 778-258-2223)
