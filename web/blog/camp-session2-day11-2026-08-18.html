JC After School · Coquitlam, BC · August 18, 2026 (Tue)

September is close now, and this week is about getting ready for it. Not just academically — getting used to the shape of a school day again after a summer of camp.

⏰ A Real Timetable on the Board Preparations

The board this morning was not a lesson plan — it was a school timetable. Bells, in effect:

9:00 amClass starts
10:30Break time
12:00Lunchtime

Simple, but it matters. After weeks of camp rhythm, sitting down when the clock says so — and getting up only when it says so — is a skill that needs a few days to come back. Better to practise it here than to rediscover it on the first morning of term.

➗ Three Levels, One Table Same time, different sheet

And here is the part worth looking at closely. Sitting side by side at the same table, working at the same time, these three worksheets were in front of three different children:

Order of OperationsBIMDAS · brackets, indices, multiplication, division, addition, subtraction — including questions with exponents
Multiplication MazesSolve each product correctly to find the path through the maze
Colour the Answer!2 + 3, 6 + 1, 3 + 3 — colour the box holding the right answer

From 108 − 12 × 9 down to 2 + 3, at one table, in the same half hour. Nobody is held back waiting for the group, and nobody is quietly lost while the group moves on. That is what "at their own level" actually looks like in practice.

Why maths puzzles, not just drills
A maze or a colouring grid gives instant feedback: get the product wrong and the path simply does not continue. The child finds their own mistake before anyone has to point it out — which is a far better way to learn than being corrected.

🍚 Lunch, Together Served, then sat down

Lunch was fried rice with freshly cut kimchi — plated up at the counter, then everybody at the table at the same time. Which, again, is the school routine we are rehearsing.
