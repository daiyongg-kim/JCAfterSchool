Field Trip Day 🚌

Field trip day! Today the whole camp headed out to Lougheed Town Centre for a visit to the Cameron Library and the mall's big indoor playground. Books, climbing, and lots of laughs — here's how our adventure went. 📚🎪

🍱 Before We Set Off Morning at the Center

We started the morning back at the center with a little learning time and a warm homemade lunch to fuel up — you need energy for a big field trip!

📚 Cameron Library Reading Adventure

Inside the library, the kids scattered to their favorite reading nooks — cozy little corners perfect for getting lost in a story. Some curled up with picture books, others hunted the shelves for their next favorite read.

🎪 Indoor Playground Climb & Play

Next stop — the mall's indoor playground! The kids raced straight for the big climbing structure, testing every twist, bar, and bouncy step. So much energy! 🤸

🛍️ Around the Mall Little Adventures

Between the library and the playground, there was plenty to see — a giant wall mural, a ride up the escalator, a peek into the shops, and a well-earned rest on the comfy chairs. 😄

What a day! Books, climbing, and adventures out in the community — the kids came back happy, tired, and already asking about the next trip. Thank you to our families for a wonderful week so far! ☀️
