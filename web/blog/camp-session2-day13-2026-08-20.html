The first presentations happened today — and the slime team did not merely talk about slime. They stood up, showed their poster, and then made it in front of everyone.

📋 The Poster First The Ultimate Guide to Slimes

Weeks of work, held up at last. Types of slime, the best recipes, what goes wrong and how to fix it — all researched, written and pasted down by the team themselves.

🧪 Then They Made It, Live Six steps, no rehearsal net

This is the part that turns a poster into a presentation. Bowl out, ingredients measured, and the recipe followed step by step while everybody watched.

3 cupsshaving cream
½ cupschool glue
1 tspbaking soda
1 tbspcontact solution
  1. Shaving cream, glue and baking soda into a large bowl.
  2. Stir together with a large spatula.
  3. Add one tablespoon of contact solution.
  4. Stir everything together.
  5. Knead and stretch with your hands until it stops being sticky.
  6. Play.

Step 5 is where a live demonstration earns its keep. The mixture comes out of the bowl sticky and unpromising, and it stays that way until enough kneading turns it — suddenly — into slime. You cannot show that on a poster.
