TGIF · Week 1 Wrap-up 🎉

Happy Friday! We closed out an amazing first week with a jam-packed day: a World Cup sports theme, a fizzy science experiment, hands-on crafts, a warm Korean lunch — and a birthday piñata celebration to top it all off. What a way to finish Week 1! 🎊

🎂

Happy Birthday, Sarah! Today we celebrated a very special birthday together — with cake, candy, and a piñata full of surprises. 🥳

⚽ Sports Day World Cup 2026 Theme

With the 2026 World Cup in the air, the morning was all about soccer — reading and vocabulary worksheets, "soccer math," coloring, and a friendly Go Fish word game. Goal! ⚽

🔬 Science Experiment Fizz & Pop!

Next up — a little chemistry magic! The kids mixed baking soda and vinegar and watched the fizz blow a balloon up all by itself. Gasps and giggles all around. 🎈

🧲 Crafts Magnets & Bracelets

Creative time meant Canada maple-leaf fridge magnets and colorful beaded bracelets — little keepsakes to take home from Week 1.

🍱 Lunch Homemade & Warm

And of course, our daily homemade Korean lunch — nutritious, delicious, and served with love. Fuel for a big afternoon! 🍚

🎉 Birthday & Piñata The Grand Finale

The best surprise came last — a birthday piñata! One by one the kids lined up, took aim, and swung for the candy inside. When it finally burst, the treats rained down to squeals of delight. 🍬

💌 A note from Teacher Diane
“That was truly an awesome week at JC summer camp!!! — including math, English, vocabulary, reading, spelling, writing, science, social studies, sports, crafts, cooking, Canada Day celebrations, a birthday celebration and piñata, a field trip, and nutritious & deliciously satisfying Korean-style lunches. I can't express enough what a great group of students we have — every single one of you. See you all on Monday!” ❤️🥰

That's a wrap on Week 1 — full of learning, laughter, and a whole lot of heart. Have a heart-warming weekend with your families, and see you all on Monday! ☀️
