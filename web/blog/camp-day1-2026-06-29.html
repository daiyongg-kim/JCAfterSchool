JC After School · Coquitlam, BC · June 29, 2026 (Mon)
Day 1 · Mon, Jun 29

It's finally Day 1 of our 2026 Summer Camp! We captured the kids' whole day — from morning lessons to lunch and afternoon play. Come along and watch them learn, laugh, eat well, and play their hearts out. 😊

🌅 Morning Class & Crafts

Mornings open with English activities led by our native-speaking teacher. One friend lost in a book, another gripping a pencil to draw — each child started the day in their own way.

Next came craft time, decorating with leaves. Gluing, coloring, and putting their heads together with friends — they poured care into every little creation.

🍱 Lunch A Warm, Homemade Korean Meal

The pride of our summer camp: a warm, homemade Korean lunch made fresh every day. Freshly cooked rice with bulgogi, kimchi, and gim (roasted seaweed) — prepared with care, just like home cooking.

The kids line up and receive their food right from the teacher before heading to their seats. Little hands carrying plates, the excitement of waiting in line — it's all part of lunchtime.

Once seated, it's time to dig in. One friend wrapping rice in gim, another holding a fork like a pro — nothing's cuter than kids eating well. 💕

🎨 Afternoon Free Play & Snacks

After a hearty lunch, it's time for some lively free play. Rolling toy cars, spinning around hand in hand, and striking a pose by the guitar. 🎸

And of course, the can't-miss snack time! Gathered together on little chairs, munching away — that's how the kids' day winds down, full and happy.

The precious children you've entrusted to us spent a safe and joyful first day. We'll keep pouring our hearts into every day of camp so each one shines just like today. Thank you for sharing them with us! ☀️
