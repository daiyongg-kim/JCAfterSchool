JC After School · Coquitlam, BC · July 8, 2026 (Wed)
Presentation Prep in Full Swing 📊

The research presentations are getting closer — and today was all about the posters! The kids warmed up with speaking questions, mind-mapped their topics, and turned their research into big colorful posters. Add a fiendish sudoku, a bulgogi lunch, an arm-wrestling showdown, and playground time — that's a full Wednesday. 💪

📋 The Game Plan Warm-up → Mindmaps → Posters

Today's whiteboard says it all: warm-up questions ("If you were…?"), a family drawing worksheet, mind maps & topics, then poster time. Structure first, creativity next!

🎨 Research Posters Birds, Bunnies & Bubble Tea

Every camper picked a topic they love and built a poster around it — “Cute Birds” (budgies and love birds!), kinds of bunnies, and even a full bubble tea research board. These will star in the upcoming presentations. 🌟

✏️ Free Drawing Pencil Artists

In between, the pencils kept moving — from a “Spooky!” character lineup to careful colored-pencil scenes. So much talent in one room!

🧩 Brain Teaser Fiendish Sudoku

And for the brave — a “fiendish”-level sudoku. Numbers 1 to 9, no repeats, no mercy. 🤓

🍱 Lunch Bulgogi Day!

Today's homemade Korean lunch: rice, kimchi, roasted seaweed, bulgogi, meat pancakes (donggeurangttaeng), and fried dumplings. A feast! 🍚

💪 Arm-Wrestling Showdown The Main Event

The afternoon's surprise hit: an arm-wrestling tournament! Cheering crowds, dramatic rematches, and very serious game faces — followed by a run-around at the playground. 🌳
